Privacy Policy for DSMS Transport

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kenilworth International ("we," "us," or "our") handles information related to the DSMS Transport / DSMS Transport mobile application (the "App"). This App is intended for exclusive use by authorized internal users of Kenilworth International and is not intended for public use.

By using the App, you acknowledge that information described in this policy—including precise location (LOCATION) data—may be collected and processed for transport operations.

Location disclosure (Google Play User Data policy)

The App accesses and collects LOCATION data. This is disclosed in detail in the “Location Data (LOCATION)” section below. Location is used only for authorized transport / trip operations and is not sold and not used for advertising.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account / authentication information: identifiers needed to sign in (for example registered official phone number for OTP authentication), name/role, and related workforce account details maintained by Kenilworth International.
  • Operational data: trip details, Transport assignments, maintenance schedules, meter readings, checklists, status updates, and related transport logs.
  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version, notification token, and diagnostic/crash data needed to run and support the App.
  • Media uploads: photos or files you choose to capture or upload for operational records (for example trip start/end photos).
  • Location data (LOCATION): precise geographic location as described below.

Location Data (LOCATION)

This App accesses LOCATION data. We disclose this clearly to comply with Google Play’s User Data / Privacy Policy requirements.

What location we access

  • Precise location (GPS and/or network-based coordinates), including latitude, longitude, and related accuracy/time metadata.
  • Location may be collected while the App is in use (foreground).
  • When a trip is active, location may also be collected while the App is in the background through a notification-visible, location-aware foreground service, so trip tracking can continue if the screen is off or another app is open.

Why we access location

  • To record trip start and end positions for operational accountability.
  • To send periodic trip location updates (“location pings”) while a trip is in progress.
  • To support dispatch, monitoring, safety, and workflow features used by authorized company staff.
  • Location is required for core transport tracking functionality. Without location permission, trip tracking features may not work.

How location is used, stored, and shared

  • Location is transmitted to our DSMS backend servers over secure connections and stored with related trip/operational records.
  • Location data is available only to authorized internal users and systems that support transport operations.
  • We do not sell location data and do not use it for advertising or marketing profiling.
  • We do not share location with third parties for marketing purposes. Sharing is limited to authorized internal teams, approved processors that host/process data on our behalf, or when required by law.

Controlling location access

  • You can grant, deny, or revoke location permission in your device settings at any time.
  • If you revoke location permission, trip tracking and related GPS-dependent features may stop working until permission is granted again.

Operational Data

The App facilitates the capture of data related to operational activities, in accordance with established company operational procedures. This includes Transport data with maintenance schedules, meter readings, and trip/location records needed for transport operations.

Access and Authentication

Access to the App is restricted to pre-registered internal users of Kenilworth International. User authentication is performed using one-time passwords (OTPs) sent to the user's registered official phone number. We do not store or retain user credentials beyond the authentication process.

How We Use Information

  • To provide transport assignment, trip tracking, and workflow features
  • To improve safety, scheduling, monitoring, and operational visibility
  • To send essential in-app or service-related notifications (including ongoing trip tracking notifications)
  • To maintain security, detect misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues
  • To comply with internal policy, audit, and legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information—including location—may be shared only with:

  • Authorized internal teams and systems within Kenilworth International that need it for transport operations
  • Approved service providers that host or process data on our behalf under contractual obligations
  • Authorities when required by applicable law or legal process

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the operational and location data captured by the App from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits
  • Secure server hosting

Data Retention

Account, operational, and location-related trip data are retained only as long as necessary for operational, legal, audit, and compliance purposes, after which they may be deleted or anonymized according to company retention rules.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date" / "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
